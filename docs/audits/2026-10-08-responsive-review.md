## Remaining access/decisions and limits

No confirmed issue from the reviewed states is intentionally left unfixed. Coverage can be extended with a sanitized representative fixture pack for high-volume records, real product/media variants and restricted staff/customer roles. The owner fixture and selected synthetic journeys do not certify every permission profile, record schema or product-photo ownership.

Real iOS/Android hardware and testers are needed for native rendering, touch input, software-keyboard/IME, VoiceOver/TalkBack, signed builds and physical accessibility acceptance. Browser sizing and keyboard checks do not certify those states.

The hermetic build deliberately omitted operator legal variables, so the public policy's **Publication requirement** variant was reviewed. Operator-verified entity/address, privacy/support contacts, retention, effective date and jurisdiction are needed to review the completed published-policy variant. This local variant does not prove production is missing those values, and no legal facts were invented.

Historical security/media/store/operational release gates were not re-audited or deployed as part of this responsive review. No overall CI, App Store or production-readiness claim follows from these checks.
